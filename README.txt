DESKWORK APK - HOW TO GET THE APP ON YOUR PHONE

1. Go to github.com and sign in (free account is fine).
2. Tap "+" > New repository. Name it deskwork. Create it.
3. Click "uploading an existing file". Drag the CONTENTS of this folder
   (www, .github, package.json, capacitor.config.json) into the page.
   If your computer hides the .github folder, turn on "show hidden files".
4. Click "Commit changes".
5. Open the "Actions" tab. Wait about 5-8 minutes for "Build APK" to turn green.
6. Click the finished run > scroll to Artifacts > download "Deskwork-APK".
7. Unzip it, send app-debug.apk to your phone, open it and tap Install
   (allow "install unknown apps" if Android asks).
